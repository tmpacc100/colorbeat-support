Privacy Policy — MediKey

Developer: Mochammad Rajah  ·  Contact: asd95179517@gmail.com  ·  Last updated: 17 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the MediKey iOS app ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum.

1. Summary

MediKey is a medical-terminology input helper. Its dictionary and your settings work on your device. We do not operate accounts or our own servers, and we do not collect, sell, or store your personal data on our side. The keyboard does not read or transmit what you type in password fields.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

  • App preferences, custom entries, and usage state — saved locally on the device and never transmitted to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as device identifiers (including the IDFA / Identifier for Advertisers), approximate location derived from IP address, device and app information, and ad-interaction/usage data.

  • Google AdMob privacy & terms: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. App Tracking Transparency (IDFA)

On iOS, the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before starting the advertising SDK. It also checks regional advertising consent using Google's User Messaging Platform. Declining tracking permission prevents ad requests; the basic dictionaries and keyboard remain available. Requests that are allowed use non-personalized advertising with publisher first-party identifiers disabled. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and any required advertising-consent choices from the app's keyboard settings screen.

5. In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment-card details or billing information.

6. Third Parties

  • Google AdMob — advertising (see section 3).
  • Apple — App Store distribution and in-app purchase processing.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your content lives on your device, deleting the app removes that data. Ad-related data handled by Google is governed by Google's own retention policies linked above.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact
